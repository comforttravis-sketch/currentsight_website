Who we work with

We'd love to hear from you if…

You're managing a fish passage or monitoring program

Whether it's a fish ladder, weir, or in-stream camera setup — if you're collecting video and want smarter analysis, that's exactly what we do.

You're a conservation organization or watershed group

We're actively building partnerships with organizations working to restore and protect aquatic habitat. Early partners help shape how the tools develop.

You're a state or federal agency managing fish populations

We understand the importance of data that holds up to regulatory scrutiny, and we design our outputs with that in mind.

You're a researcher who needs better population data

Consistent, repeatable counts from video analysis can strengthen fieldwork and reduce observer variability in your datasets.

You manage a hatchery or water resource facility

If fish passage or population data plays a role in your operations, we'd like to understand how automated monitoring could help.
